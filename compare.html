Planet vs Star Comparison

🪐 Planet vs ☀️ Star Comparative Explorer

At first glance, both planets and stars are spherical celestial bodies orbiting through galaxies. But fundamentally, they occupy entirely different realms of physics and chemistry: stars are colossal engines of thermonuclear fusion burning ionized plasma, while planets are cooled, non-fusing worlds composed of solid rock, liquids, and molecular gases.

Select any planet (from our Solar System or distant exoplanet systems) and any star to inspect their side-by-side differences in composition, scale, internal physics, and lifespan!
